Built with EN 1176-certified components

Each project combines durable materials, vibrant design and a wide range of play elements such as slides, ball pools, climbing towers, swings, tunnels and interactive zones. From a family corner in a café to a full-scale entertainment zone, our solutions are scalable and built to last.

All components are manufactured within 3–4 weeks and delivered to your location. Every product comes with full certification and a 24–36 month warranty.

Our services include

  • Consultation to find the best solution for your space
  • Custom product design tailored to your brand and layout
  • 3D visualization of the proposed playground
  • Personalized colour schemes and thematic styling
  • Transportation and on-site installation
  • Certification, documentation and safety checks
  • Ongoing inspections and annual maintenance
