Our story

From distributor to manufacturer

Since our founding in 2008, REATEK has grown from a trusted supplier of inflatable products into a leading manufacturer of custom-built entertainment and advertising solutions. In 2010, we expanded into the indoor playground segment.

We operate across Europe and specialize in safety (EN 1176, EN 14960), professional design and tailored solutions for small and medium-sized enterprises.
