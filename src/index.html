Our story

From distributor to manufacturer

Since 2008, REATEK has grown from a trusted supplier of inflatable products into a leading manufacturer of custom-built entertainment and advertising solutions. In 2010, we expanded into indoor playgrounds.

We operate across Europe and specialize in safety (EN 1176, EN 14960), professional design and tailored solutions for small and medium-sized enterprises.
