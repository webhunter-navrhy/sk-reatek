About us

REATEK EUROPE Group

Since 2008 we have grown from a trusted supplier of inflatables into a manufacturer of custom entertainment and advertising solutions. In 2010 we expanded into indoor playgrounds.

What we create

  • Amusement inflatables with branding
  • Advertising inflatables
  • Pneumatic tents, arches and furniture
  • Indoor playgrounds for hotels and entrepreneurs

Our journey began as a distributor of selected European inflatable brands. By listening to customer needs, we moved to developing tailor-made inflatables designed for the European market.

Why REATEK

We specialize in bespoke solutions for small and medium-sized enterprises and offer a 24–36 month warranty. Our vision is to become a leading European supplier of inflatable advertising products and premium indoor playgrounds.

Active member of IAAPA since 2016: we regularly attend global exhibitions to stay ahead of trends and technologies.
